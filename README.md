### ✈️ **2. Flight Price Prediction (PRCP-1025)**  
**Category:** Machine Learning — Regression  

**📘 Problem Statement:**  
Developed a machine learning model to predict **flight ticket prices** based on variables such as airline, source, destination, total stops, and duration.

**🎯 Objectives:**  
- Clean, preprocess, and engineer features from flight data.  
- Train regression models to estimate ticket prices accurately.  

**📊 Outcome:**  
Based on the performance metrics, **LightGBM** is the most suitable model for production deployment because:
- It achieves the best balance between precision, recall, and F1 score.
- It has the highest AUC score (0.89), making it highly capable of distinguishing between customers who are likely to buy the product and those who are not.
- The high precision (0.6465) ensures that false positives are minimized, which is important when targeting customers for a marketing campaign.
- It offers competitive accuracy (0.9075), ensuring a large portion of predictions are correct.  

**🧰 Techniques Used:**  
Feature Engineering 🧩 | Regression Models 📉 | Model Tuning ⚙️ | Data Visualization 📊

### 🏦 **3. Portuguese Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis (PRCP-1000)**  
**Category:** Classification | Exploratory Data Analysis  

**📘 Problem Statement:**  
Analyzed a dataset from a Portuguese bank’s marketing campaign to determine factors influencing a customer’s decision to subscribe to a term deposit.

**🎯 Objectives:**  
- Perform **EDA** to uncover customer behavior patterns.  
- Build classification models to predict deposit subscription (`yes` / `no`).  

**📊 Insights:**  
- Factors such as **age**, **job type**, and **previous campaign outcome** strongly influenced subscription rates.  

**🧰 Techniques Used:**  
EDA 🔍 | Classification 🤖 | Feature Engineering 🧮 | Data Visualization 📊
